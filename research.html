Research

I use theoretical and computational chemistry to understand how enzymes control chemical reactions: the electric fields that proteins exert on their active sites, how those fields shape the electronic structure of metal centers, and how they steer the quantum dynamics of electron and proton transfer.

  1. IElectric fields in enzymes
  2. IIElectronic structure in strongly correlated systems
  3. IIIQuantum charge transfer
