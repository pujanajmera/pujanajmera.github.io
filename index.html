    Ongoing Projects

    Here are a few things I’m currently working on:

    • Methods for electric fields in enzymes: Developed pycpet, (GitHub), to evaluate dynamics of 3D electric fields and electrostatic potentials in active sites
    • Electrostatic control of electronic structure in strongly correlated systems: Working on interesting metalloenzymes with complex electronic structure and understanding the role of the protein environment
    • Electrostatics in controlling quantum charge transfer processes: Modelling electrostatic control of dynamic PCET and electron transfer.
