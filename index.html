Pujan Ajmera

PhD Candidate, Theoretical & Computational Chemistry – UCLA

Google Scholar · GitHub

Welcome! I’m a PhD candidate at UCLA in the Alexandrova Lab. My research focuses on theoretical and computational modeling of how enzymes exert control over electronic structure through electrostatics (+ dynamics!).

I’m currently working on methods for analyzing electric field dynamics and their charge density interactions in enzymes, understanding electrostatic control of electronic structure in strongly correlated systems, and determining the role of electric fields in controlling charge transfer. My collaborative work with experimentalists surrounds designing metalloenzymes, often informed by our theoretical advancements in understanding electrostatic preorganization. More on my research.
